# AI Study Tool Figure plan

## Size

**Figure**: one mechanism, 80 purposeful parts, shaded isometric SVG, with a single animated retrieval carriage. One figure is embedded in the AI Study Tool case study.

## Concept

The **local study bench** feeds course files into an index, slides a retrieval carriage to a source card, and carries that context into a local model before placing a grounded quiz or note on the output tray.

The carriage represents passage retrieval: selecting a relevant source passage and moving it toward the local model. The selector is a schematic of the documented RAG workflow, not a claim about internal app telemetry.

## Facts represented

- The project accepts PDF, TXT, DOCX, and PPTX source material.
- Its documented approach uses Sentence Transformers, ChromaDB, LangChain, and a locally run Mistral model through Ollama.
- The study outputs are quizzes and notes grounded in uploaded sources.
- No chunk counts, embedding dimensions, relevance scores, latency, or accuracy are documented here, so the figure will not show those numbers.

## Interaction and presentation

- The three existing workflow steps remain available as buttons: add material, retrieve context, and review outputs.
- Selecting a step eases the carriage to its physical position and highlights only the corresponding mechanism.
- An idle tour walks the same three stages, pauses for user input, stops offscreen, and stays still when reduced motion is requested.
- The card has a live readout, legend, truthful caption, keyboard controls, and touch-safe operation.
- The composition stays dark with subdued graphite solids; cyan marks the single active part, with violet used only as a small secondary signal.
